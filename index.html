BROWSER ARCADE

Coin Rush Arena

A party game that runs entirely in the browser — no app, no install, no server. Put the game on a TV or laptop, then everyone joins from their phone and uses it as a controller. First to grab the most coins wins.

📺

Host a game

Open this on the big screen — TV, laptop, projector. It shows a QR code and a 4-digit room code for players to join.
🎮

Join as a player

On your phone: scan the QR on the host screen, or type the 4-digit code. Your phone becomes the controller.

How it works

1. On the big screen, tap Host a game — a room code and QR appear.
2. On each phone, open the camera and scan the QR, or go to this site and tap Join and type the code.
3. Move with the on-screen joystick, tap the button to dash, and race to collect coins before the timer runs out.

Works on Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox. Players connect peer-to-peer over WebRTC — the phone and the screen talk directly, so latency stays low.
